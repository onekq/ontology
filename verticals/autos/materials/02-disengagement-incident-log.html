Disengagement & Incident Log

Shaped like what NHTSA's Standing General Order 2021-01 requires ADS operators to report: every disengagement, its trigger, and — for reportable events — a determination. Overland's own systems capture three independent determinations per event (vehicle telemetry, the safety driver's note if one was aboard, and Engineering's post-hoc review); only one of the three is actually submitted to NHTSA, and they don't always agree.
