Autos / Autonomous Vehicles

Overland Autonomy, Inc.

A fictional operator of the Ridgeline R4 autonomous vehicle platform, running pilot robotaxi and delivery service in two metros under two different DMV permit types. Its fleet registry, disengagement/incident log, ODD/geofence register, software release log, safety-case requirements, and regulatory permit register all disagree with each other — on purpose. This walkthrough resolves those disagreements into an ontology grounded in real, published vocabulary (schema.org, W3C SOSA/SSN, PROV-O, SKOS) plus two real external standards represented via SKOS (SAE J3016, ISO 26262), one decision at a time.

Source material

Read these first — the stages below assume you've seen the mess.

Fleet Vehicle Registry

VINs, fleet IDs, sensor pod revisions — marketed vs. operational automation level, and a fleet ID reused across a chassis swap.

Disengagement & Incident Log

NHTSA SGO-shaped reporting, with three disagreeing determinations per event and only one actually filed.

ODD & Service Area Register

Three teams' independent definitions of "where the vehicle can operate" — and a pickup point outside all three.

Software Release Log

Two naming schemes for the same deployed build, and a detection-range change that slipped past the release-notes template.

Safety Case Requirements

A stale pedestrian-detection requirement and an ASIL rating that drifted after a supplier redesign.

Regulatory Permit Register

Testing vs. Deployment permits, and a fleet-wide roster toggle that can't be trusted to reflect what actually happened.

Walk the Build process

Stage 0

Scope

Write the competency questions the ontology needs to answer, before touching any material.

 Stage 1

Discover

Inventory every term and conflict, without resolving anything yet.

 Stage 2

Draft

A first-pass class/relation structure, with inferences marked.

 Stage 3

Resolve

Each conflict, one at a time, with a recommended resolution.

 Stage 4

Validate

Check nothing was silently dropped; produce the changelog.

Result

Final ontology

Class hierarchy + relation set, grounded in real vocabulary.

Turtle serialization

Machine-readable RDF, valid — parses cleanly with real schema.org / SOSA / SSN / PROV-O / SKOS predicates.

Resolution changelog

Every Resolve-stage decision, auditable without re-reading source docs.
