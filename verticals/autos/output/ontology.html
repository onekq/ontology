Autos / Autonomous Vehicles — Result

Overland Autonomy — Resolved Ontology

Output of the Build method applied to materials/, grounded in schema.org's Vehicle vocabulary, W3C/OGC SOSA/SSN, W3C PROV-O, and W3C SKOS — the last used both for cross-reference confidence and to represent two real external controlled vocabularies (SAE J3016 automation levels, ISO 26262 ASIL ratings) as queryable concept schemes. See Stage 3 — Resolve for the reasoning, and the changelog for a flat list of decisions. Machine-readable: ontology.ttl (raw markdown: ontology.md).

Worked instances

  • VIN ...100111 / ...100558 — both denoted by fleet ID FLT-0231, at non-overlapping time ranges
  • Build 4.12.2 / perception-v7.3.1 bundle — two Identifiers, skos:exactMatch
  • INC-0442 — three prov:Attributions; engineering review's carries regulatoryFilingOf = true
  • REQ-AV-014 — requirementSatisfiedBy perception-v7.2.0, requiresReverification = true
  • REQ-AV-022 — two ASIL-Rating claims (ASIL-D, ASIL-C), both retained, neither preferred
  • Ashcombe Commons — no match property to either OperationalDesignDomain or PermitBoundary; route produces a PermitBoundaryExcursion
  • FLT-0244 — Permit.safetyDriverRequired = true, but safetyDriverPresent = false on 12 of 20 recent trips
  • Arterial road class, Dunmore — two SpeedEnvelopeObservations (45mph, 35mph), kept as two results

Known gaps

PermitBoundaryExcursion has one worked instance (Ashcombe Commons/Colby Bridge) — no other route has been checked at the segment level fleet-wide yet.

REQ-AV-022's ASIL disagreement is deliberately left unresolved, not defaulted to either rating — the ontology's job is to make the conflict blocking and visible, not to substitute for a real safety review.
