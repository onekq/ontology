Competency questions, re-checked

Both gaps flagged in Stage 0 are now answerable: the ontology can represent whether a specific trip's full route stayed inside its permit boundary (Conflict 8), and it can represent observed safety-driver presence separately from roster policy defaults (Conflict 9). Neither gap required new source material beyond what materials/06 already contained; both required relations (SOSA observations, domain PermitBoundaryExcursion) the earlier fleet/engineering-only pass never had reason to use.
