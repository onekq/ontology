Conflict 1 of 10
Parent decision — resolve first

Are 89432CA0010001 and 89432CA0010002 the same plan or different ones?

Same SERFF filing, different cost-sharing variation. Nothing cross-indexes them as a family.

Recommended — schema.org + domain
Keep each as its own real HealthInsurancePlan instance — they are legally distinct products with distinct HIOS IDs — linked by a domain PlanFamily grouping, since schema.org has no "variation of" relation. Same shape as the Product/ProductGroup and Work/Expression splits in prior verticals.
Conflict 2 of 10

HIOS Plan ID, internal code, and SERFF tracking number don't always coexist

Bronze PPO has no SERFF number at all yet, but is already being quoted.

Recommended — schema.org
Keep all three independent — schema.org's own usesHealthPlanIdStandard property implies HIOS is a preferred, not exclusive, standard. Leave the missing SERFF number absent rather than inferred; it's a real, meaningful gap given Conflict 8.
Conflict 3 of 10 — highest stakes

Thistlebrook's formulary tier and the PBM's actual billing tier disagree

Atorvastatin: Tier 2 in Thistlebrook's official record, Tier 3 at the pharmacy counter after Meridian Rx's 2025 contract change.

Recommended — schema.org + domain
Keep healthPlanDrugTier (Thistlebrook's official value) and add a domain PBMTierRecord for Meridian Rx's actual billing value, flagged tierMismatch: true — don't silently trust either as correct. This is the conflict with real financial stakes for members being billed incorrectly.
Conflict 4 of 10

Coinsurance timing disagrees between the plan spec and the claims system

Spec: after deductible. Claims system default for this service category: before deductible, a 2023 configuration error.

Recommended — schema.org
Assert healthPlanCoinsuranceOption: after deductible per the real, filed plan spec — that's the authoritative source. Flag the claims system's default as a domain claimsConfigMismatch: true needing correction, not an equally valid alternate reading.
Conflict 5 of 10

A provider is billed in-network after their contract ended

HealthPlanNetwork record: in-network. Provider Relations: terminated 2025-04-01.

Recommended — schema.org + domain
Give the HealthPlanNetwork membership a domain effectiveThrough date matching Provider Relations' real contract end, and flag any claim processed after that date as processedAgainstStaleNetworkStatus: true — surfacing the specific claims affected, not just the general policy gap.
Conflict 6 of 10

Does "Plan" mean anything quotable, or only an approved product?

Sales quotes pre-filing plans as if they were final. Compliance reserves the term for approved, HIOS-assigned plans.

Recommended — schema.org + domain
Sales' meaning becomes a domain QuotableOffering; Compliance's stays the real HealthInsurancePlan, which a QuotableOffering is only promoted to once SERFF approval and HIOS assignment both complete. Same "loose commercial term vs. strict legal term" pattern as Offer/Agreement and Party/LegalPerson in prior verticals.
Conflict 7 of 10 — same pattern as Legal's Manifestation drift

Marketing brochures still show last year's rates

benefitsSummaryUrl/healthPlanMarketingUrl point at 2023 documents; the 2024 rate change isn't reflected.

Recommended — schema.org + domain
Give the linked document a domain lastGenerated date, flagged stale relative to the plan's most recent approved rate-change date — the same "public rendering drifts from canonical source" shape as the legal vertical's posted-PDF conflict, now confirmed as a genuinely recurring cross-domain pattern, not a one-off.
Conflict 8 of 10

Nothing distinguishes "Filed" from "Approved"

The registry records a SERFF tracking number as soon as one exists — Bronze PPO has none at all and is already being sold.

Recommended — domain-specific
Add a domain SERFFFilingStatus (Filed/Approved/Rejected) as its own tracked value, distinct from merely having a tracking number. Neither ACORD's gated specs nor schema.org's health-plan terms cover regulatory approval workflow — checked first, then invented deliberately, same discipline as every prior vertical's domain-specific fallback.
Conflict 9 of 10

The renewal checkpoint doesn't reconcile formulary tiers

"Confirm plan documents updated" verifies regeneration, not that the underlying tiers are actually current.

Recommended — domain-specific
Add a domain reconcilesFormularyTiers: false flag on the checkpoint — same cross-domain checkpoint-gap pattern as every prior vertical (manufacturing's QC check, retail's returns inspection, finance's KYC refresh, healthcare's care-plan review, media's attribution duty, legal's records request).
Conflict 10 of 10

The published hold-time SLA doesn't hold during open enrollment

Published: under 5 minutes, year-round. Actual, October–December: 12 minutes average.

Recommended — domain-specific
Model two domain ServiceLevelClaim instances — the published year-round figure and the actual seasonal one — rather than reconciled into a single number, same principle as every prior vertical's operational-disagreement conflict.
