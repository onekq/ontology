# Plan Renewal & Customer Service Process Notes

Maintained by Product Operations and Member Services respectively.

## Annual Plan Renewal Review Checklist

1. **Trigger.** Renewal cycle begins (90 days before plan-year end).
2. **Confirm plan documents updated.** Verify brochures and summaries are regenerated for the
   new plan year. *(Does not verify the formulary's drug tiers were reconciled against the
   Pharmacy Benefit Manager's current tiers — only that documents were regenerated, which can
   faithfully reproduce an already-stale tier.)*
3. **File for next plan year.**

## Member Services Call Center SLA

Published standard (member handbook): **"Average hold time under 5 minutes."**

Internal call center reporting (reviewed monthly): average hold time during open-enrollment
season (October–December) is **12 minutes**. The published figure has never been updated to
reflect seasonal variation — it reads as a year-round claim.
