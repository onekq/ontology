Sales & Compliance Glossary Snapshot

As maintained separately by two teams. No single glossary exists. (raw export)

Sales/Broker wiki — "Plan"

Anything a broker can quote to a prospective member, including plans still pending state filing approval. Sales calls it "the Bronze plan" as soon as pricing is set internally.

Compliance/Actuarial wiki — "Plan"

A HealthInsurancePlan with an approved SERFF filing and an assigned HIOS Plan ID — legally marketable. A plan pending filing is not a Plan in this sense yet, regardless of what Sales calls it.
