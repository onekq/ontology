Insurance

Thistlebrook Health Plans

A fictional health insurer. Its plan registry, formulary/cost-sharing records, network/provider status records, sales/compliance glossary, regulatory filing requirements, and renewal/customer-service process notes all disagree with each other — on purpose. This walkthrough resolves those disagreements into an ontology grounded in real, published vocabulary (schema.org's health-insurance terms, created for a real 2015 CMS mandate) — centered on a realistic formulary-tier billing discrepancy.
