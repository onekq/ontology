Thistlebrook Health Plans — Resolved Ontology

Output of the Build method applied to materials/, grounded in schema.org's health-insurance vocabulary — created in response to a real 2015 CMS regulatory requirement for machine-readable health plan data. See Stage 3 — Resolve for the reasoning, and the changelog for a flat list of decisions. Machine-readable: ontology.ttl (raw markdown: ontology.md).

Why schema.org, not ACORD

ACORD is the dominant real insurance data-exchange standard, but its specs are largely membership-gated XML message schemas, not openly published RDF/OWL. A separate check of insuranceontology.com found it real but thin and still in development. schema.org's CMS-driven health-plan vocabulary was the strongest openly-verifiable option — narrower (health insurance specifically) but genuinely real and well-documented.

Known gaps

This vertical covers health insurance specifically — schema.org's real vocabulary doesn't extend to general P&C, life, or commercial lines. A vertical for those would need ACORD directly (with its access constraints) or another source not yet identified.

Full picture, including which conflicts have a worked example in ontology.ttl, is in the changelog.
