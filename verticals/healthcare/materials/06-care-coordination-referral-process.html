Care Plan Review Checklist

1. Trigger. Review due date reached (per care plan schedule).
2. Contact patient. Phone or portal message.
3. Confirm patient contacted.
Does not require reconciling the medication list against what the patient reports currently taking.
4. Update care plan.
5. File.
