Clinical & Billing Glossary Snapshot

As maintained separately by two departments. No single glossary exists. (raw export)

Nursing wiki — "Active"

The patient is currently admitted — there is an open encounter for them right now.

Billing/Coding wiki — "Active"

A condition's clinical status is "active," meaning it is currently considered present and relevant — regardless of whether the patient is currently admitted anywhere.

Front Desk wiki — "Encounter"

Any scheduled visit slot on the calendar, whether or not the patient showed up.

Clinical/Billing wiki — "Encounter"

A visit that actually happened. A no-show never becomes an Encounter record — it's cancelled before it would be created.
