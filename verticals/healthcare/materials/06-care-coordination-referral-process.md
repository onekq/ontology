# Care Coordination & Referral Process Notes

Maintained by Care Coordination.

## Care Plan Review Checklist

1. **Trigger.** Review due date reached (per care plan schedule).
2. **Contact patient.** Phone or portal message.
3. **Confirm patient contacted.** *(Does not require reconciling the medication list against what
   the patient reports currently taking.)*
4. **Update care plan.**
5. **File.**

## Referral SLA

Scheduling's stated turnaround: **"Referrals processed within 3 business days."**

Specialty clinic intake's own internal tracking (reviewed quarterly): average time from referral
receipt to first available appointment offered is **6 business days**. The two figures have never
been reconciled — Scheduling owns the stated turnaround, the specialty clinic owns its own intake
data, and neither reviews the other's number.
