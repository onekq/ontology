# Consent & Compliance Requirements Register (excerpt)

Maintained by Compliance. Each requirement has a "Satisfied by" field set once and rarely
revisited.

## REQ-301 — Data sharing consent
> Sharing a patient's record with the external specialty referral network requires an active
> consent on file covering the "care coordination" purpose of use.

**Satisfied by:** Consent record CONSENT-401.

CONSENT-401 was obtained in 2020, scoped to "billing" purpose only — not "care coordination."
Referral sharing has been proceeding under a consent that doesn't actually cover it.

## REQ-302 — Medication reconciliation at care plan review
> Every care plan review must include a reconciliation of the patient's current medication list.

**Satisfied by:** the care plan review checklist's "confirm patient contacted" checkpoint.

That checkpoint confirms contact happened — it does not require the medication list to actually
be reconciled against what the patient reports taking.

## REQ-303 — Duplicate-record verification before merge
> Two matching identifiers must be confirmed before two patient records can be flagged as
> duplicates of each other and merged.

**Satisfied by:** the registration system's automated duplicate-check flag.

The system flagged MRN-100234 and MRN-100891 as a possible duplicate in 2023 (matching DOB, SSN
last 4, and insurance member ID — well past the two-identifier bar). The flag was never actioned
by staff, and the records remain unmerged.
