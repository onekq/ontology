Conflict 1 of 10
Parent decision — resolve first

Does "Customer ID" identify the legal entity, or the account relationship a Relationship Manager services?

Both meanings are in active use across the core banking system and the CRM.

Recommended — FIBO + domain
Keep LegalEntity/LegalPerson (FIBO) as the identity, and give the RM's book of accounts its own domain class, AccountRelationship, distinct from it. Same shape as the retail vertical's SKU/Offering split — most downstream conflicts depend on this distinction already being made.
Depends on Conflict 1

LEI, Tax ID, and internal Customer ID don't always all exist for "the same" entity

Bramwell has no Tax ID. Fennimore has no LEI. Individuals have neither.

Recommended — FIBO
Keep all three as independent properties on LegalEntity — FIBO itself doesn't require an LEI for every legal person (LEIs are only mandatory for certain regulated transactions). Leave genuinely absent identifiers absent rather than inferring them; flag with identifierCompleteness, same discipline as the retail vertical's missing-MPN case.
Conflict 3 of 10 — highest stakes

Larkspur's beneficial ownership is recorded only to the direct layer, not the ultimate one

Bramwell Capital Partners LP holds 60% of Larkspur directly. Bramwell's own controlling individuals were never identified — REQ-201 requires identifying anyone with 25%+ effective ownership, not just direct ownership.

Recommended — FIBO, used precisely
Assert Bramwell isDirectlyConsolidatedBy Larkspur — that's a true, known fact. Do not assert isUltimatelyConsolidatedBy for anyone — it's genuinely unknown, and inventing a plausible-sounding ultimate owner would be worse than leaving the gap visible. Classify Bramwell as a SpecialPurposeVehicle specifically so "an SPV with no traced ultimate owner" becomes a queryable, flaggable pattern — this is the exact distinction FIBO's two consolidation properties exist to make, and using only the "direct" one here is what real KYC failures actually look like.
Conflict 4 of 10

Larkspur's jurisdiction is stale

Core system: Delaware, set at 2019 onboarding. Actual, per 2024 Secretary of State filing: Nevada.

Recommended — FIBO
Model jurisdiction and EntityLegalForm as point-in-time facts with their own effective dates, not a single mutable field — the 2019 Delaware record and the 2024 Nevada record are both real, just at different times. The underlying process problem (legal entity data flows from onboarding, never from an ongoing state-filing feed) is a data-pipeline fix, not something the ontology itself can correct — but the ontology should at least stop presenting a single stale field as current fact.
Conflict 5 of 10

Does "Relationship" mean an RM's book of accounts, or a formal ownership link?

Relationship Management and Compliance use the same word for unrelated concepts.

Recommended — already settled in Draft
AccountRelationship (domain, from Conflict 1) for Relationship Management's meaning; FIBO's isDirectlyConsolidatedBy/isUltimatelyConsolidatedBy family for Compliance's. No new class needed at Resolve time — Draft already separated them for a different reason, and that separation happens to resolve this conflict too.
Conflict 6 of 10

Does "Party" mean any CRM contact, or a KYC-complete entity?

Most CRM "parties" haven't been screened at all.

Recommended — FIBO + domain
A domain ProspectContact class for the CRM meaning, promoted to FIBO's LegalPerson/LegalEntity only once KYC and screening actually complete. FIBO's own notion of a legal person already implies a real, identified entity — it was never meant to cover an unscreened prospect, so the CRM's looser concept needs its own class rather than being folded into FIBO's.
Conflict 7 of 10

Two interest rates are simultaneously on file for the same loan

RATE-1 (7.25%, open-ended) and RATE-2 (6.85%, 2023-06-01 to 2023-12-31) both show as active during the overlap window.

Recommended — domain-specific
FIBO's interest-rate/pricing modules weren't researched deeply enough here to ground this in — rather than guess at a FIBO fit, use a domain RateRecord class with its own effective-date range, same honest fallback as manufacturing's alternativeTo and retail's DeliveryEstimate. Keep both records distinct; which one actually applied during the overlap is a servicing/legal question the ontology surfaces but doesn't resolve.
Conflict 8 of 10

Loan approvals are only auditable by a human reading a note

Approver and timestamp live in a free-text notes field, not structured data.

Recommended — W3C PROV-O
Model each approval as a prov:Activity with prov:wasAssociatedWith (the approver) and prov:endedAtTime — real, structured, queryable provenance, replacing the by-convention text note. PROV-O is domain-neutral, reused here the same deliberate way SKOS and PROV-O were reused in manufacturing and retail, not because FIBO lacks an audit concept but because provenance genuinely isn't FIBO's job.
Conflict 9 of 10 — depends on Conflict 3

The annual KYC refresh checkpoint doesn't re-check beneficial ownership

"Confirm still operating" passes regardless of whether the ownership structure changed — or, as with Larkspur, was ever fully traced to begin with.

Recommended — domain-specific
Add a reverifiesBeneficialOwnership: false flag on this checkpoint step, same pattern as manufacturing's QC-checkpoint and retail's returns-inspection gaps — a named checkpoint that sounds like it covers something doesn't mean it does, and the ontology's job is to make that explicit rather than let the checkpoint's name imply more than it checks.
Conflict 10 of 10

Compliance's risk rating and Transaction Monitoring's flag disagree

Compliance: "Low" risk, annual review. Transaction Monitoring: an unreviewed flag noting a transaction pattern inconsistent with Larkspur's stated business purpose.

Recommended — domain-specific
Model the risk rating and the monitoring flag as two separate, timestamped assessments rather than reconciling them into one status — same principle as manufacturing's cycle-time conflict and retail's SLA conflict. An unreviewed flag contradicting a stale "Low" rating is itself the fact worth surfacing, not something to silently average away.
