A naming collision between two real ontologies

FIBO has its own BusinessEntity class (in BE/LegalEntities) — a completely different real ontology than GoodRelations, which also has a gr:BusinessEntity (used in the retail vertical for sellers). Same name, two unrelated real vocabularies, two different meanings. Worth flagging explicitly: "it's from a real ontology" isn't by itself enough to disambiguate a term — which real ontology matters just as much as whether it's real at all.

Where FIBO doesn't reach

FIBO models legal and ownership structure exhaustively but has no notion of "a Relationship Manager's book of accounts," "a free-text approval note," or "a transaction-monitoring flag." Those need domain-specific vocabulary, checked against FIBO first rather than assumed absent.
