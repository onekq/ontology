Worked instances

  • Larkspur Holdings LLC (LegalEntity) — isDirectlyConsolidatedBy ← Bramwell Capital Partners LP (60%), Marcus Feld (25%), Sonia Feld (15%); two jurisdiction records (Delaware 2019, Nevada 2024)
  • Bramwell Capital Partners LP (SpecialPurposeVehicle) — no isUltimatelyConsolidatedBy asserted; the gap itself is the point
  • Loan KB-88291 — two RateRecords, kept distinct
  • Approval Activity — wasAssociatedWith the approver, endedAtTime the decision timestamp
  • KYC refresh checkpoint — reverifiesBeneficialOwnership: false
  • Two risk assessments — Compliance's "Low" rating and Transaction Monitoring's unreviewed flag, kept separate

Known gaps

isUltimatelyConsolidatedBy is intentionally absent for Bramwell — this is the central finding of the whole vertical, not an oversight. A future update should add it only once Kestrel's compliance team actually identifies Bramwell's controlling individuals.
