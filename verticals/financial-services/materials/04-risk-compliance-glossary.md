# Risk & Compliance Glossary Snapshot

As maintained separately by two departments. No single glossary exists.

## Relationship Management wiki — "Relationship"
> A Relationship Manager's assigned book of customer accounts. "Managing the relationship" means
> handling day-to-day servicing and sales for those accounts.

## Compliance/KYC wiki — "Relationship"
> A formal, recorded link between two legal entities — an ownership stake, a consolidation link,
> a guarantor arrangement. Bramwell Capital Partners LP's 60% stake in Larkspur Holdings LLC is
> a "relationship" in this sense. Has nothing to do with which employee services the account.

## Relationship Management / CRM wiki — "Party"
> Any contact record in the CRM — including prospects who inquired but never became customers.

## Compliance/KYC wiki — "Party"
> A legally identified and screened entity — has completed KYC, sanctions screening, and (for
> organizations) beneficial ownership identification. Most CRM "parties" are not compliance
> "parties" yet, and the two systems don't cross-reference which is which.
