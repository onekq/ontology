# KYC Refresh & Loan Approval Process Notes

Maintained by Compliance Operations.

## Annual KYC Refresh Checklist

1. **Trigger.** Refresh due date reached (365 days since last refresh).
2. **Confirm still operating.** Verify business is active (state registration lookup, site visit
   or call). *(Does not re-verify beneficial ownership — a business can pass this check while its
   ownership structure has changed entirely, or was never fully traced in the first place.)*
3. **Update risk rating.** Analyst reviews file, assigns risk tier.
4. **File.**

## Loan Approval Recording

Approval decisions are recorded in the loan system's `status` field (Approved/Declined) plus a
free-text `notes` field where the approver writes their name and date by convention — not a
structured, queryable approver/timestamp pair. There is no system-enforced requirement that the
note actually contain this information.
