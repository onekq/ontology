Eight verticals, one method

This is the eighth and final vertical in the original plan. Across manufacturing (IOF Core), retail (GoodRelations/schema.org), financial services (FIBO), healthcare (FHIR), media/real estate (ODRL), legal (Akoma Ntoso), insurance (schema.org's health-plan terms), and government (W3C ORG + CPSV-AP), the same four-shape pattern kept recurring even though every vocabulary, company, and conflict was different: a checkpoint whose name promises more than it checks; a canonical fact that drifts from a public rendering of it; two teams' operational claims that disagree with neither being wrong; and a parent identity conflict that, resolved first, fixes one or more dependent conflicts for free. That recurrence across eight unrelated real ontologies is itself a finding, not a coincidence — see ../../../methodology.html for the method these verticals share.
