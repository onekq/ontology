Conflict 1 of 10
Parent decision — resolve first

Are SVC-2201 and SVC-2201-ON the same service?

Same underlying service, two channels — but the online channel silently doesn't support the veteran fee-waiver code the in-person channel does.

Recommended — CPSV-AP, used precisely
One PublicService, with each delivery method as its own real CPSV-AP Channel. Add a domain channelRequirementVariance flag where a requirement (like the fee waiver) isn't uniformly supported across channels — the gap should be visible to whoever maintains the online form, not just discovered by a veteran being charged full price.
Conflict 2 of 10

Org unit code, state registry ID, and federal grant ID don't always coexist

ORG-ECON-01 has no federal grant ID yet.

Recommended — W3C ORG
Keep all three independent on the OrganizationalUnit — ORG doesn't require any particular identifier scheme. Leave the missing grant ID absent rather than inferred.
Conflict 3 of 10 — highest stakes

The service catalog still points citizens to the wrong department

The 2024 reorg updated the org chart and phone directory. It never updated hasCompetentAuthority on either service record.

Recommended — CPSV-AP + W3C ORG, used together
Update hasCompetentAuthority on both service records to ORG-ECON-01, matching the real subOrganizationOf/hasSubOrganization change in the org hierarchy. Beyond this one fix, add a general integrity check: any PublicService whose hasCompetentAuthority wasn't touched within a reasonable window of a relevant org-hierarchy change should be flagged — same "systemic check, not just a one-off fix" discipline as legal's activeModifications/passiveModifications integrity check.
Conflict 4 of 10

A requirement and its evidence are written as if they were two requirements

"Must be a resident" and "must provide a utility bill" are listed as equals, when the second only proves the first.

Recommended — CPSV-AP, no invention needed
Model residency as a real Requirement and the utility bill as one accepted real Evidence instance for it — with a lease agreement as a second, equally valid Evidence instance for the same Requirement. CPSV-AP's own Requirement/Evidence split fixes this conflict directly; it was never a modeling gap, only a documentation one.
Conflict 5 of 10

"30 days of the triggering event" doesn't say which event date counts

For a BusinessEvent (ownership change), staff have used both the sale date and the state-registration date in practice.

Recommended — CPSV-AP
LifeEvent and BusinessEvent are already distinct real classes — the ambiguity was never about modeling, only about which date the BusinessEvent instance's own timestamp should record. Recommend the state-registration date specifically, since it's objectively verifiable and independently timestamped, unlike a privately-agreed sale date.
Conflict 6 of 10

Does "Department" mean a real org unit or a website category?

Website categories don't map 1:1 to real organizational units — some span several, some split one.

Recommended — W3C ORG + domain
HR's meaning stays the real OrganizationalUnit. The website's meaning gets a domain NavigationCategory class, explicitly many-to-many linked to OrganizationalUnit — documenting the actual overlap/split pattern instead of leaving it implicit and untracked.
Conflict 7 of 10 — the fourth confirmed instance of this pattern

The linked Rule document still shows the old fee

Generated 2022 at $50; the Board approved $65 in 2025. Never regenerated.

Recommended — CPSV-AP + domain
Give the Rule document a domain lastGenerated date, flagged stale against the Board's approved change date — the same "canonical fact updates, public rendering doesn't" shape as manufacturing's work-instruction/BOM drift, legal's Manifestation conflict, and insurance's marketing brochure conflict. Four unrelated domains, same failure mode — confirmed pattern, not coincidence.
Conflict 8 of 10

The catalog's Cost doesn't match Finance's fee schedule

Catalog: $50. Finance: $65, same 2025 increase.

Recommended — CPSV-AP
Treat Finance's fee schedule as canonical for Cost, and model the catalog's value as derived from it with a staleness flag when they diverge — same "one system is definitionally authoritative" reasoning as media's royalty-percentage and legal's jurisdiction conflicts, not a "keep both" case.
Conflict 9 of 10

The annual review checkpoint doesn't reconcile authority or cost

"Confirm service description reviewed" verifies reading, not reconciling.

Recommended — domain-specific
Add a domain reconcilesCompetentAuthorityAndCost: false flag on the checkpoint — the same cross-domain checkpoint-gap pattern that's now appeared in every single vertical this project has built.
Conflict 10 of 10

The published processing time doesn't hold during renewal season

Published: 5 business days, year-round. Actual, January–March: 11 business days average.

Recommended — domain-specific
Model two domain ProcessingTimeClaim instances — published and actual, seasonal — rather than reconciled into one figure, the same operational-disagreement pattern as every prior vertical's SLA conflict.
All ten resolved. Next: Stage 4 — Validate.
