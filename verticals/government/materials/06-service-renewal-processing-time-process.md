# Service Renewal & Processing Time Process Notes

Maintained by the County Clerk's office and the Department of Commerce respectively.

## Annual Service Catalog Review Checklist

1. **Trigger.** Annual review cycle begins.
2. **Confirm service description reviewed.** *(Does not require reconciling competent
   authority, cost, or rule-document currency against Finance, HR, or the actual reorg order —
   only that someone read the existing text.)*
3. **File for next cycle.**

## Business License Processing Time

County website's published standard: **"Licenses processed within 5 business days."**

Department of Commerce's internal tracking (reviewed monthly): average processing time during
renewal season (January–March, when most licenses come due) is **11 business days**. The
published figure has never been updated to reflect seasonal volume — it reads as a year-round
claim.
