# Requirements & Evidence Records — Business License Renewal (SVC-2201)

## Requirement/Evidence conflation

The service description lists, as a single undifferentiated bullet list of "requirements":

- "Must be a county resident or have a registered business address in the county."
- "Must provide a utility bill dated within the last 60 days."

The first is a genuine **Requirement** (a condition that must hold). The second is not a separate
requirement — it's the **Evidence** used to prove the first one. The documentation doesn't
distinguish them, so staff sometimes reject an applicant who proves residency a different way
(e.g., a lease agreement) because "the requirement" as written names the utility bill
specifically.

## Event-triggered requirement ambiguity

A second listed requirement: "Renewal must be filed within 30 days of the triggering event."

For a **LifeEvent** trigger (e.g., a sole proprietor's address change), "the triggering event" is
the address change date. For a **BusinessEvent** trigger (e.g., a change of business ownership),
staff have interpreted "the triggering event" differently in practice — sometimes the sale date,
sometimes the date new ownership was formally registered with the state, which can be weeks
apart. The requirement text never specifies which.
