# Competent Authority & Reorganization Records

## 2024 Reorganization Order (excerpt)

Effective 2024-07-01: Business licensing functions, previously under the Department of Commerce
(ORG-COMM-04), transfer to the newly created Department of Economic Development (ORG-ECON-01).
Staff, budget lines, and physical office space transferred per the order.

## What was and wasn't updated

- **Org chart (HR system):** Updated. ORG-ECON-01 shows business-licensing staff; ORG-COMM-04
  no longer does.
- **Service catalog (`hasCompetentAuthority` on SVC-2201 / SVC-2201-ON):** **Not updated.** Both
  service records still list ORG-COMM-04.
- **Phone directory:** Updated to route business-licensing calls to ORG-ECON-01's line.

Citizens who use the service catalog's listed contact (still ORG-COMM-04) are being misdirected,
even though the phone directory itself was correctly updated. The two systems disagree because
only one of them was touched during the reorg rollout.
