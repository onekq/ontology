# Department & Navigation Glossary Snapshot

As maintained separately by two teams. No single glossary exists.

## HR/Org Chart wiki — "Department"

> A formal `OrganizationalUnit` in the county's organizational hierarchy — has its own budget
> line, staff roster, and reporting structure.

## County Website team wiki — "Department"

> A navigation category on the public-facing website, used to group related services for citizen
> convenience. Some website "departments" span multiple real organizational units (e.g.,
> "Business Services" on the website includes services from both the Department of Commerce and
> the Department of Economic Development); others split one real organizational unit's services
> across two website categories. Neither mapping is documented anywhere.
