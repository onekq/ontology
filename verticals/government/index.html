Government

Elmsworth County

A fictional county government. Its public-service/org registry, reorganization records, requirements/evidence records, department/navigation glossary, rule/cost documentation, and renewal-processing-time notes all disagree with each other — on purpose, centered on a 2024 departmental reorganization that the service catalog never caught up with. This walkthrough resolves those disagreements into an ontology grounded in two real, actively-maintained standards: the W3C Organization Ontology (ORG) and the Core Public Service Vocabulary Application Profile (CPSV-AP) — the eighth and final vertical in this project.

Walk the Build process

Stage 0

Scope

Write the competency questions the ontology needs to answer, before touching any material.

 Stage 1

Discover

Inventory every term and conflict, without resolving anything yet.

 Stage 2

Draft

A first-pass class/relation structure, using ORG and CPSV-AP precisely.

 Stage 3

Resolve

Ten conflicts, one at a time, with a recommended resolution.

 Stage 4

Validate

Check nothing was silently dropped; produce the changelog.

Result

Final ontology

Class hierarchy + relation set, grounded in W3C ORG and CPSV-AP, with a refinement disclosed mid-build.

Turtle serialization

Machine-readable RDF, valid — with both real CPSV/CPSV-AP namespaces used correctly.

Resolution changelog

Every Resolve-stage decision, auditable without re-reading source docs.
