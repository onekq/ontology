Retail / E-commerce

Alderleaf

A fictional home & outdoor goods e-commerce marketplace. Its product catalog, marketplace offers feed, promotions log, category taxonomy, storefront requirements, and returns/ fulfillment process all disagree with each other — on purpose. This walkthrough resolves those disagreements into an ontology grounded in real, published vocabulary (GoodRelations, schema.org, PROV-O, SKOS), one decision at a time.

Source material

Read these first — the stages below assume you've seen the mess.

Product Catalog

SKU, GTIN, and MPN fields — and a variant whose spec quietly went stale after a manufacturer redesign.

Marketplace Offers Feed

Three sellers, one GTIN, and one seller whose own condition field contradicts its own description.

Promotions Log

Two promotions with overlapping validity windows on the same variant.

Category Taxonomy & Glossary

A post-merger category crosswalk, and a term ("Listing") two teams define differently.

Storefront Requirements

Three requirements whose "Satisfied by" fields have quietly stopped being true.

Returns & Fulfillment Process

An inspection checkpoint that can't catch a substituted item, and two teams' disagreeing SLA numbers.
