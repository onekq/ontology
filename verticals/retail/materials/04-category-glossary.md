# Category Taxonomy & Glossary Snapshot

Two artifacts, neither reconciled against the other.

## Category crosswalk (post-merger)

Alderleaf acquired TrailMart's catalog in 2025. The migration script assigned new category codes
to TrailMart's products but did not touch Alderleaf's existing catalog — so the same kind of
product now lives under two different trees depending on which system it originated in.

| Product | Legacy tree (pre-2025, Alderleaf) | Current tree (post-2025 code) |
|---|---|---|
| Cascade Trail Backpack | Camping > Backpacks | Outdoor Gear > Hiking > Packs (`OUT-HIK-PACK-01`) |
| Trailmix Cook Pot 1.5L | Camping > Cookware | Kitchen > Cookware > Camp (`KIT-COOK-POT-01`) |

Legacy BI dashboards still report against the old tree names. Nobody has re-pointed them.

## Marketing wiki — "Listing"
> The storefront page for a product — one per product (or product group), regardless of how
> many sellers offer it.

## Marketplace Ops wiki — "Listing"
> A specific seller's offer for a product — one per seller per product, meaning a single
> storefront page can correspond to several "listings" in this sense (see Marketplace Offers
> Feed: three sellers, three listings, one storefront page).
