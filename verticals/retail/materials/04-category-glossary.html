Category crosswalk (post-merger)

Alderleaf acquired TrailMart's catalog in 2025. The migration script assigned new category codes to TrailMart's products but did not touch Alderleaf's existing catalog — so the same kind of product now lives under two different trees depending on which system it originated in.

ProductLegacy tree (pre-2025)Current tree (post-2025 code)
Cascade Trail BackpackCamping > BackpacksOutdoor Gear > Hiking > Packs (OUT-HIK-PACK-01)
Trailmix Cook Pot 1.5LCamping > CookwareKitchen > Cookware > Camp (KIT-COOK-POT-01)

Legacy BI dashboards still report against the old tree names. Nobody has re-pointed them.

Marketing wiki — "Listing"

The storefront page for a product — one per product (or product group), regardless of how many sellers offer it.

Marketplace Ops wiki — "Listing"

A specific seller's offer for a product — one per seller per product, meaning a single storefront page can correspond to several "listings" in this sense (see Marketplace Offers Feed: three sellers, three listings, one storefront page).
