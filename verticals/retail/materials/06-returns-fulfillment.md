# Returns & Fulfillment Process Notes

Maintained by Warehouse Ops. Revision tracking on this document covers process steps only, not
kept in sync with Marketing's storefront copy.

## Returns Processing Checklist

1. **Receive return.** Log tracking number, match to original order.
2. **Inspect.** Confirm item present, box present. *(Does not verify the returned item's SKU/GTIN
   matches the original order — a wrong or substituted item passes this checkpoint identically to
   a correct one.)*
3. **Disposition decision.** Resellable-as-new → restock. Opened/used → refurbish-and-relist as
   "Refurbished." Damaged → dispose.
4. **Process refund.**

## Fulfillment SLA

Marketing's storefront copy: **"Ships within 1 business day."**

Warehouse Ops' internal capacity note (updated quarterly): average processing time for
non-priority orders is **1.5 business days** during standard staffing. The two figures have never
been reconciled — Marketing owns the storefront copy, Warehouse Ops owns the capacity model, and
neither reviews the other's number.
