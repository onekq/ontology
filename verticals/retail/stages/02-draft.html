A different real pattern than manufacturing

The manufacturing vertical grounds in IOF Core, which reifies identifiers as their own Identifier entities that denote things (a BFO-derived pattern). GoodRelations and schema.org do not do this — sku, gtin13, and mpn are plain datatype properties directly on a product or offer. Copying the manufacturing pattern here would be grounding in the wrong tradition for this domain. The right move is to use whichever real ontology's actual pattern fits — not to reuse the previous vertical's pattern by default.

Classes adopted

ClassSourceDefinition
Product / ProductGroupschema.orgThe abstract item and its variant-bearing group. Cascade Trail Backpack is a ProductGroup; each capacity/color combination is a Product that isVariantOf it.
OfferingGoodRelations"The public... announcement by a BusinessEntity to provide... a certain ProductOrService." One per seller per product — this is the formal name for what Marketplace Ops calls a "listing."
BusinessEntityGoodRelationsThe seller making an Offering — Alderleaf Direct, TrailGearCo, OutdoorLiquidators are each one.
AggregateOfferschema.orgRolls up multiple Offerings for the same product into a price range — this is what a storefront page (Marketing's "listing") actually displays.
UnitPriceSpecificationGoodRelationsA price with validFrom/validThrough — covers both promotions directly.
OfferItemConditionschema.orgEnumeration: NewCondition, RefurbishedCondition, UsedCondition. A real, already-standard vocabulary for the condition conflict — nothing to invent here.

Two items reuse patterns from the manufacturing vertical (SKOS, PROV-O) because those are domain-neutral W3C vocabularies — that's different from reusing IOF Core, which is manufacturing-specific and deliberately not reused here.
