Competency questions, re-checked

Both gaps flagged in Stage 0 are now answerable: the ontology can represent that Station 2's checkpoint doesn't verify rotor identity (Conflict 9), and it can represent — rather than silently resolve — the cycle-time disagreement between Production Engineering and Production Planning (Conflict 10). Neither gap required new source material beyond what materials/06 already contained; both required IOF Core classes the earlier BOM-only pass never had reason to use.

Not resolved — logged, not dropped

Classification codes: partial coverage was noted in Discover but never taken to Resolve — there's no conflict to adjudicate, just a data-completeness gap. Recording that explicitly here is the point: Validate exists to catch things that got quietly skipped, and "skipped for a legitimate reason" still has to be written down, not just skipped again.

REQ-009: folded into Conflict 8's fix rather than resolved separately — a real modeling call, not an oversight, since both are instances of the same "satisfied-by-link-goes-stale" defect.
