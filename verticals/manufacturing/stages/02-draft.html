Candidate classes

ClassDefinition
ProductA top-level sellable unit (e.g. MTR-100).
StructuralAssemblyA BOM node with children, regardless of how it's purchased. [inferred] — split from Procurement's "assembly" meaning, not yet confirmed as the right split.
PurchasedKitA unit bought as one line item from a supplier. [inferred] — same split as above.
PartA leaf-level component (no children in the BOM).
SupplierCrossReferenceA supplier's own part number/description mapped to an internal part. [inferred] — not an explicit concept in the source docs, but implied by the cross-reference table.
RevisionA dated snapshot of a part's design.
DepartmentRoleA named job function (e.g. Quality Controller). [inferred] — added specifically to disambiguate from the electronics "Controller" part.
