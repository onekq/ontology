Stage 0 — Scope

Before reading a single source document, write the questions the finished ontology needs to be able to answer — competency questions (Grüninger & Fox 1995, popularized by Noy & McGuinness). This is the field's actual standard first move. Skipping it is how an ontology ends up internally tidy but unable to answer the question anyone actually needed answered.

What the two gaps mean

Everything answerable above came from the BOM, supplier catalog, change log, and requirements register — all catalog-shaped source material. Neither gap can be closed by that material, because neither is about what parts exist — they're about the process that builds them and whether that process actually checks what it's supposed to check. That's not a coincidence: it's why materials/06-assembly-work-instruction exists. Stage 1 picks up from here.
