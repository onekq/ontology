# Internal Glossary Snapshot (as maintained separately by three departments)

No single glossary exists. These are excerpts from each department's own wiki page, never reconciled.

## Engineering wiki — "Assembly"
> Any BOM node with one or more children. A subassembly can itself contain subassemblies. Purely structural — says nothing about how or whether the thing is purchased as a unit.

## Procurement wiki — "Assembly"
> A kitted or pre-packaged unit purchased as a single line item from a supplier. Several of Engineering's "assemblies" (e.g. STA-200) are not purchased as a kit at all — they're built in-house from individually sourced parts, so Procurement doesn't track them as an "assembly," just as a build instruction.

## QA wiki — "Controller"
> Job role: Quality Controller, floor-level inspection sign-off authority. (See also: Controller Board Assembly, an unrelated electronics part — Engineering's ECN-124 renamed it "Motor Control Unit" specifically to stop this collision, but the old name persists in ERP.)

## Procurement wiki — "Housing"
> Outer shipping carton/crate used for logistics and freight quoting. (See also: Motor Housing HSG-500, the cast aluminum enclosure — an unrelated physical part that engineering also just calls "the housing.")

## Engineering wiki — "Rev" / "Revision"
> A dated snapshot of a part's design. Revisions are assumed to supersede one another (Rev C replaces Rev B). This assumption is false for the Rotor Subassembly specifically — Rev B and Rev C are concurrently shipping in different product lines (see ECN-118) — but the glossary entry hasn't been updated to carve out that exception.
