Manufacturing / Supply Chain

Cairn Drivetrain Co.

A fictional mid-drive motor manufacturer for e-bikes. Its bill of materials, supplier catalog, engineering change log, and department glossary all disagree with each other — on purpose. This walkthrough resolves those disagreements into an ontology, one decision at a time.
