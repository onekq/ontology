Known gaps

UsageEvent, ComplianceCheck, and PaymentRecord are domain classes, not ODRL ones — ODRL models rights and obligations, not monitoring/reconciliation processes. A real boundary of what the ontology covers, not an oversight.

Full picture, including which conflicts have a worked example in ontology.ttl, is in the changelog.
