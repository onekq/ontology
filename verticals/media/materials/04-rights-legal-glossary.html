Rights & Legal Glossary Snapshot

As maintained separately by two departments. No single glossary exists. (raw export)

Sales wiki — "License"

Any deal a client has agreed to, verbally or in a signed term sheet, including deals still pending final legal signoff. Sales tells clients "you're licensed" as soon as terms are agreed.

Rights/Legal wiki — "License"

A fully executed Agreement with countersigned terms on file. A verbally agreed or pending-signature deal is not a License in this sense — it's a Policy of type Offer at best, not yet an Agreement.
