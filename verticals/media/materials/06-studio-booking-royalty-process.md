Maintained by Studio Operations and Finance respectively.

## Studio Space Booking Checklist

1. **Request.** Client or internal team requests studio space for a shoot window.
2. **Check calendar.** Facilities calendar reviewed for conflicts. *(Only checks the facilities
   team's own calendar — does not cross-check the rights-management system's own studio-use
   agreements, which are booked separately by the Sales team for client shoots.)*
3. **Confirm booking.**
4. **File.**
