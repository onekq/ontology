# Attribution & Compliance Requirements Register (excerpt)

Maintained by Rights/Legal. Each requirement has a "Satisfied by" field set once and rarely
revisited.

## REQ-401 — Attribution duty fulfillment
> Any Agreement whose terms include a Duty to attribute the creator must have that attribution
> confirmed as actually appearing in the published work.

**Satisfied by:** the Duty clause existing on the Agreement.

The Duty being present in the contract is not the same fact as the credit actually appearing.
No Amberlane process checks published work against the attribution requirement after signing.

## REQ-402 — Territory constraint enforcement
> Licensed usage must be confirmed to stay within the Agreement's spatial constraint before
> renewal.

**Satisfied by:** the Agreement's own `spatial` field.

The field states the constraint; nothing confirms usage was ever checked against it prior to
the 2025 compliance audit finding for POL-9001.

## REQ-403 — Royalty percentage consistency
> The royalty percentage owed to a creator under a given Agreement must be the same figure in
> Finance's payment system and Rights/Legal's contract file.

**Satisfied by:** the Agreement's `payAmount`/`percentage` terms as filed at signing.

No process reconciles Finance's running payment calculations against the original filed terms
after signing.
