Legal

City of Thornfield

A fictional municipal legal code office. Its ordinance registry, amendment log, codification/cross-reference records, legal/clerical glossary, publication requirements, and records-request process notes all disagree with each other — on purpose. This walkthrough resolves those disagreements into an ontology grounded in real, published vocabulary (Akoma Ntoso / LegalDocML, an OASIS standard) — the first vertical in this project grounded in an XML-native standard with no official RDF binding, disclosed explicitly rather than glossed over.

Walk the Build process

Stage 0

Scope

Write the competency questions the ontology needs to answer, before touching any material.

 Stage 1

Discover

Inventory every term and conflict, without resolving anything yet.

 Stage 2

Draft

A first-pass class/relation structure, using FRBR's real layering precisely.

 Stage 3

Resolve

Ten conflicts, one at a time, with a recommended resolution.

 Stage 4

Validate

Check nothing was silently dropped; produce the changelog.

Result

Final ontology

Class hierarchy + relation set, grounded in real vocabulary — with an honest note on its RDF mapping.

Turtle serialization

Machine-readable RDF, valid — parses cleanly with real Akoma Ntoso element/attribute names.

Resolution changelog

Every Resolve-stage decision, auditable without re-reading source docs.
