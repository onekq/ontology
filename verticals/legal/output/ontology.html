City of Thornfield — Resolved Ontology

Output of the Build method applied to materials/, grounded in Akoma Ntoso (LegalDocML, OASIS standard). See Stage 3 — Resolve for the reasoning, and the changelog for a flat list of decisions. Machine-readable: ontology.ttl (raw markdown: ontology.md).

A different kind of "real" than prior verticals

IOF Core, GoodRelations, FIBO, FHIR, and ODRL are all natively RDF/OWL. Akoma Ntoso is not — it's an XML Schema vocabulary (real namespace, verified: http://docs.oasis-open.org/legaldocml/ns/akn/3.0), with no official RDF/OWL binding. This Turtle file maps its real element/attribute names into RDF as this project's own mapping, not an existing standard one — a bigger disclosure than FHIR's "simplified triple shape," since there's no official RDF version to simplify from here.
