# Codification & Cross-Reference Records

## Renumbering history

TMC Title 14 (Public Nuisances) was recodified in 2020 — sections were renumbered to
accommodate new chapters. TMC 14.20.030 (Noise Control Standards) was previously numbered
14.08.030 before recodification.

## Stale cross-reference

TMC 14.20.030's own text contains a cross-reference: "as defined in Section 14.08.010." That
citation used the *pre-2020* numbering. Section 14.08.010 no longer exists under that number —
the definitions it referred to are now at TMC 14.20.010. The reference was never updated during
recodification.

## Manifestation drift

The City website's posted PDF of TMC 14.20.030 was last regenerated in 2022. It does not reflect
the subsection (b) decibel-limit change made by Ordinance 2023-07. The canonical XML record
(the current Expression) is correct; the publicly posted Manifestation is not.
