# Legal & Clerical Glossary Snapshot

As maintained separately by two offices. No single glossary exists.

## City Clerk's wiki — "Code"
> The entire consolidated Thornfield Municipal Code — every codified ordinance currently in
> force, as one body of law.

## Code Enforcement wiki — "Code"
> The specific section cited on a citation or violation notice. An officer who says "you're in
> violation of the Code" means one section, not the whole document, and expects the reader to
> know which one from context.

## City Clerk's wiki — "Section"
> A numbered division of the codified Municipal Code (e.g. TMC 14.20.030).

## Records Office wiki — "Section"
> A tab or divider in a physical or digital filing folder, unrelated to the Municipal Code's
> numbering.
