Public Records Request & Codification Process Notes

Maintained by the Records Office and the Codification team respectively. (raw export)

Public Records Request Checklist

1. Receive request. Log requester and requested document.
2. Locate document. Pull from the records archive.
3. Confirm document provided.
Does not verify the pulled document is the current Expression — the archive is organized by ordinance filing number, and an older cached printout can be filed alongside the current one without a clear "superseded" marking.
4. Close request.
