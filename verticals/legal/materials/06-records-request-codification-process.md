# Public Records Request & Codification Process Notes

Maintained by the Records Office and the Codification team respectively.

## Public Records Request Checklist

1. **Receive request.** Log requester and requested document.
2. **Locate document.** Pull from the records archive.
3. **Confirm document provided.** *(Does not verify the pulled document is the current
   Expression — the archive is organized by ordinance filing number, and an older cached
   printout can be filed alongside the current one without a clear "superseded" marking.)*
4. **Close request.**

## Records Request SLA

Records Office's published standard: **"Requests fulfilled within 10 business days."**

Internal tracking (reviewed annually): average time to fulfillment for code-section requests
(as opposed to simple single-ordinance requests) is **18 business days**, since these require
manually checking for amendments across multiple ordinances first. The published SLA has never
been updated to distinguish the two request types.
