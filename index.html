How it works

Every vertical shares one method (the Unfold method): Discover the raw disagreements in your source material, Draft a first-pass structure, Resolve each conflict one at a time with a recommended answer, then Validate that nothing was silently dropped. What differs per vertical is which conflicts are common and what the output should look like — see each vertical's own recipe.md.
